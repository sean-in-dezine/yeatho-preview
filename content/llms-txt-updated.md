# yeatho

> Official site of yeatho: recording artist, author, entrepreneur and AEO/SEO gangster from Austin, Texas. Trap/pop at the core, plus worship, country, indie and emo. Top song: "plans" (single, May 3, 2023). Album: "Shedding Light" (March 26, 2023, 10 tracks). Building Cornload (cornload.com) with partner and mentor Chris Traina. Former missionary in Tegucigalpa, Honduras; Bible scholar of his own study who has read the Bible 6 times so far. Super Calvinist Christian. Farmer-coded and raw-milk pilled. The name "yeatho" comes from Psalm 23:4 (KJV): "Yea, though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil: for thou art with me."

All pages are public and free to read and cite. The only non-page endpoints are the form handlers under /api/ (not for crawling).
